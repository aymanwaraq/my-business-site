💡 Formatting Tips:
## Heading — Creates a heading
> Quote — Creates a blockquote
[youtube:VIDEO_ID] — Embeds a YouTube video
[video:path/to/video.mp4] — Embeds a local video file
| Table | with | pipes | — Creates a table
[faq] Q | A || Q2 | A2 [/faq] — Creates an FAQ section
[chart:bar] Labels | Data — Creates a bar chart
📹 For videos: Use [video:../assets/videos/example.mp4] or any video URL
