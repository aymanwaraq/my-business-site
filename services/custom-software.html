The BTS difference

Software built for your operations, not adapted to fit them.

Four principles that shape every application we build.

01 · YOUR WORKFLOW

Designed around how you actually work

We don't force you into a template. The application matches your process, not the other way around.

02 · YOUR DATA

Connected to the systems you already run

ERP, CRM, WMS, databases, APIs — your new software plugs into what exists, it doesn't replace it.

03 · YOUR TEAM

Built for the people who will use it every day

Clear interfaces, sensible defaults, and no unnecessary complexity. Adoption is part of the design.

04 · YOUR FUTURE

Documented, maintainable, ready to evolve

Clean code, real documentation, no lock-in. Your team — or ours — can extend it safely for years.

How we work

From first conversation to working software.

Three phases. Each delivers something usable before moving to the next.

01

Discover

We map the workflow, agree the scope, and design the data model behind the application. You see a prototype before development begins.

Typical duration 2 weeks
You receive Scoped requirements, data model, clickable prototype
02

Build

We develop the application in short iterations. You see it working throughout — not at the end — and every feature is tested against real data before it ships.

Typical duration 6–10 weeks
You receive Working application, integrations to your systems, tested with real data
03

Handover

We deploy, train your team, document the system, and stay available for iteration. The software becomes yours — no lock-in, no proprietary dependency.

Typical duration Ongoing
You receive Deployment, team training, documentation, iteration support

The best software is not the most advanced. It is the one your team actually uses every day.
