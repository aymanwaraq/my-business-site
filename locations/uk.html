For UK Businesses Working With the Gulf

If your organisation works with Gulf suppliers, partners, or customers, you face a specific set of operational challenges:

  • Bilingual requirements — documents, communications, and systems must work in both English and Arabic
  • Cross-border documentation — customs forms, bills of lading, and compliance records in two languages
  • Cultural context — understanding how business is actually done in Gulf markets
  • Systems integration — connecting UK platforms with Gulf partners' systems

BTS is built for this reality. Our work is designed specifically for organisations operating in the space between the UK and the Gulf.
