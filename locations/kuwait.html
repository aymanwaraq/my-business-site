Kuwait Is Modernising

Kuwait has one of the strongest economies in the Gulf, with significant financial reserves and a mature enterprise sector. New Kuwait 2035 outlines the country's long-term vision for economic diversification and digital transformation — creating growing demand for technology across banking, energy, logistics, and government services.

BTS works with Kuwaiti organisations navigating this transformation. We deliver AI, software, and bilingual Arabic-English systems designed for the operational realities of Kuwaiti businesses.
