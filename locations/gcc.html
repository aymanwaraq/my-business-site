The Gulf Cooperation Council

The Gulf Cooperation Council consists of six major economies: Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain. Together, these countries represent one of the world's most important regions for infrastructure investment, digital transformation, and enterprise innovation.

Each country has its own regulatory context, business culture, and technological priorities. But they share common features that make the GCC a distinct and important market:

  • Rapid digital transformation driven by national visions and government investment
  • Growing demand for bilingual Arabic-English technology
  • Strong focus on AI, automation, and operational efficiency
  • Increasing cross-border trade with the United Kingdom and Europe
  • Sophisticated enterprise buyers with high expectations
