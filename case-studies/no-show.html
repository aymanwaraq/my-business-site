📋 No-Show Prediction (Healthcare)

Client Problem
GP clinics face 15-20% missed appointments, wasting clinical capacity, increasing wait times, and costing the NHS over £1 billion annually. Existing scheduling systems cannot identify high-risk patients who are likely to miss their appointments.

Solution
A Random Forest machine learning model was developed using synthetic NHS appointment data (150,000 records). The model predicts patient no-show probability based on age, wait days, deprivation quintile, and prior attendance history. A threshold of 0.161 was selected to prioritise recall (80%), enabling targeted SMS reminders and risk-based overbooking.

Results
✔ 28.7% relative reduction in no-show rates (23.0% → 16.4%)
✔ 7.3 percentage point increase in clinician utilisation (59.8% → 67.1%)
✔ 13.1:1 return on investment (£21,603 net benefit per GP annually)
✔ No negative impact on patient waiting times

Technologies
Python · Scikit-learn · Random Forest · SimPy · Pandas · Fairlearn
