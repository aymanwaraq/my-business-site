Why Many Dashboards Fail

Many organisations already have dashboards. The problem is that they are often:

  • Historical only — showing yesterday's news, not today's reality
  • Manual to update — creating additional workload for already busy teams
  • Overloaded with metrics — overwhelming users with data rather than insight
  • Lacking context — presenting numbers without explaining what they mean
  • Disconnected — not integrating with operational systems

Traditional dashboards answer the question: "What happened?" Intelligent dashboards go further — answering: "What is happening right now?" — and predict: "What is likely to happen next?"
