The Problem With Manual Document Processing

Businesses in the UK and GCC exchange enormous volumes of paperwork. Invoices. Purchase orders. Customs forms. Bills of lading. Contracts. Delivery notes. Compliance records. Every one of these documents must be read, verified, entered into a system, and routed to the right person.

Done manually, this work:

  • Consumes hours of skilled employees' time every week
  • Introduces human error — especially in repetitive data entry
  • Slows down operations because nothing moves until the paperwork does
  • Gets worse as the business grows (more volume = more staff = more cost)

For organisations operating across the UK and GCC, this problem is amplified. Documents arrive in both English and Arabic, often in the same file, frequently with different formats, layouts, and regulatory contexts.

The Bilingual Advantage

Most document AI systems are built for English-first markets. Arabic support is often an afterthought — poor recognition of Arabic text, confusion with mixed Arabic-English documents, and difficulty with regional document formats.

BTS builds document intelligence systems where Arabic and English are treated equally:

  • Accurate recognition of Arabic text — including right-to-left layouts and diacritics
  • Handling of mixed Arabic-English documents in a single file
  • Recognition of regional document formats — GCC customs forms, Saudi invoices, UAE compliance documents
  • Bilingual output: extracted data available in either language

This matters because documents crossing the UK-GCC corridor rarely stay in one language.

How It Works

1. Document Ingestion

Documents arrive via email, upload, scanned image, or system integration. Our systems accept PDFs, scans, photos, and digital files in any format.

2. AI Extraction

Our AI models read the document — recognizing text, tables, signatures, and structured fields — in both Arabic and English. The extraction is trained on your document types, not just generic layouts.

3. Validation

Extracted data is validated against business rules. Missing fields, inconsistent values, duplicate records, and anomalies are flagged automatically.

4. Routing & Integration

Clean data flows into your ERP, CRM, accounting, or warehouse systems. Documents are routed to the right person or queue based on content. Only exceptions reach a human.
