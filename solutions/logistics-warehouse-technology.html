Technology for Cross-Border Logistics

Logistics operations between the UK and the Gulf are complex. Goods cross multiple borders, documentation arrives in different languages, and coordinating parties are often spread across time zones and regulatory environments. Off-the-shelf software was rarely designed for this reality.

BTS builds logistics and warehouse technology specifically for organisations operating across these environments. Our work focuses on three practical realities of modern UK-GCC trade:

  • Operations happen in both English and Arabic — interfaces, documents, and communications must work in both.
  • Documentation is heavy and regulatory — customs forms, bills of lading, compliance records, and delivery notes must be captured, validated, and stored accurately.
  • Coordination is fragmented — operators, drivers, brokers, and warehouse staff often work through different systems and channels.

We design systems that respect these realities rather than fighting against them.
